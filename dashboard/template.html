Status. Hold payouts: any click without a landing, or ≥ 3 orders with half or more from flagged clicks. Review: ≥ 20% of clicks flagged, or any order from a flagged click. OK: neither. Publishers with fewer than 10 clicks and no flagged click are marked low volume.

Strong indicators: click without landing · in the brand's cart or checkout within the look-back · on the brand's site, untagged, within the look-back · publisher-bought search ad. Medium: brand search or coupon / cash-back site in the 60 s before. Review: landings on 2+ other advertisers within ±2 min. Weak: no activity in the 30 min before.

US estimates = panel count × the brand's scale factor (calibrated on Similarweb). Commission at risk = flagged orders × scale × average order × commission × 365. Defaults: Walmart $112.50 and 2.5% (AOV $100–125, commission 1–4%); mattress brands $860 and 5% (Saatva AOV, commission 3–10%). One day of panel data: treat small counts as leads, not verdicts.
